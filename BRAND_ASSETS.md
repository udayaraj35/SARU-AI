# Brand assets

`brand-*.png` are rendered from the corresponding SVGs in [Simple Icons 16.0.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0/icons). Source distribution: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.0.0/icons/ . Simple Icons uses CC0-1.0; individual trademarks belong to their respective owners. The bundled files are brand silhouettes in their associated colors (white for dark-background monochrome marks). They are not endorsements or integrations by those brands.

`creator_photo.jpg` is the application creator portrait supplied by Udaya Raj Khanal for this app. Owner photos are stored separately in the local SARU data directory and are not included in source releases.
